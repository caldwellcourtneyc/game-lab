HOW TO PLAY

Tap or click any orb to pop it and all connected same-colored orbs.

Scoring: Chains of N orbs score N × N × 10 points.
But popping a single orb costs 10 points, so build chains!

10 Stages with escalating score targets.
Hit the target to clear the stage. New colors and faster drops each stage.
Board resets between stages. Score carries over.

Special orbs:
Flashing white = Bomb (clears 3×3 area)
Rainbow cycling = Rainbow (matches any color)
Chains of 8+ leave behind a bonus bomb.

Game over if any column fills to the top.
